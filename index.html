About Us

Treasure Chest Self Storage is a locally owned and managed storage facility located at 111 Lower Elm Street in downtown Macon, Georgia. We help families, students, and small businesses find convenient storage solutions for their everyday needs.

Our facility offers climate-controlled storage units, Noke keyless entry, individual unit alarms, and camera monitoring. Whether you need extra space during a move, a place for seasonal belongings, or storage for business supplies, our friendly team is ready to help you choose the right option. Visit our official website to learn more about our services.

We also make moving and shipping easier with U-Haul rentals, packing supplies, and shipping options from FedEx, UPS, USPS, and DHL. Our goal is to bring helpful services together in one convenient location and make your storage and moving experience as simple as possible.
